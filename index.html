The Purple Blossom — Our Story

Born in a small backyard greenhouse, The Purple Blossom began as a weekend hobby that bloomed into a purpose: to bring gentle beauty and calm into people's lives using the language of flowers.

Origin: The shop began when our founder, Mira, cultivated a patch of rare purple varieties. Neighbors fell in love with her bouquets and soon asked her to craft arrangements for celebrations and quiet moments alike.

Mission: To create handcrafted bouquets that celebrate tenderness, connection, and thoughtful living. Each arrangement is ethically sourced, hand-tied, and sent with a meaningful note.

Why purple? Purple symbolizes grace, mystery, and devotion. Our collections use shades of lavender, mauve, and violet to convey calm, admiration and timeless romance. That purple, being the last color in the rainbow spectrum, signifies that they will love and trust each other for a long, long time.

In life & love: Flowers are reminders — of care, of memory, of courage. We design bouquets to be keepsakes, small acts of beauty that make everyday moments feel special.
